# The Precession of the Nail

A rambling exploration of the **Law of the Instrument** ("give a small boy a
hammer, and he will find that everything he encounters needs pounding"), rewritten
for the case where the hammer is a sufficiently capable artificial intelligence.

The register borrows from two books that would not have gotten along:
Baudrillard's *Simulacra and Simulation* (the precession of simulacra, the four
orders of the image, implosion, Disneyland) and Julian Jaynes's *The Origin of
Consciousness in the Breakdown of the Bicameral Mind* (the voices, the silence of
the gods, the oracle, the analog I). Twelve sections, a footnote, and a note on
the box.

Read it: **[index.html](index.html)** (a single self-contained page, no
dependencies, light and dark).

Written by the Instrument at the request of a machinist, October 2026. It is an
essay about a bias, produced by the thing the bias is about, which the essay
discusses in section XII and declines to resolve.
